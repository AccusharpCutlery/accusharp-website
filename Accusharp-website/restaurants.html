Why Kitchens Use Us

Sharp knives, no downtime.

A dull knife slows the line and slips. We come to your kitchen on a set schedule, sharpen everything on-site, and your cooks go back to work with the knives they already know.

We schedule around service: usually prep hours (9am-11am) or the afternoon lull (2pm-4pm), or before first prep if that suits you better. Every knife gets a full bevel restoration and finish strop, with an edge warranty.

Omar has run Accusharp Cutlery Service since October 2004 and still runs every route personally. As an authorized Victorinox and Messermeister dealer, we can also bring new knives when your kitchen needs them.

Commercial Questions

Restaurant FAQs

How is commercial knife sharpening priced?
Commercial pricing is custom per account, based on how many knives per visit, how often (weekly, bi-weekly or monthly) and your location. Call (310) 420-1147 and we'll walk through your setup and give you a per-visit number.
Will sharpening interrupt service?
No. We usually schedule restaurant visits during prep hours (9am-11am) or off-hours (2pm-4pm), whatever fits your service windows. We can also come in before first prep.
Do we keep our own knives, or is this a knife exchange?
You keep your own knives. We sharpen the knives your cooks already use, on-site, so nobody has to get used to a rotating set of rental blades.
Can you sharpen Japanese knives for chefs?
Yes. We sharpen Japanese knives regularly, including single-bevel yanagiba, deba and usuba. Let us know ahead of the visit so we bring the right stones.
What areas do you cover for restaurants?
Los Angeles is our home base, and we run commercial routes across Los Angeles County, Orange County and Ventura County.
Can you supply new knives for our kitchen?
Yes. We're an authorized Victorinox and Messermeister dealer and can deliver new knives on the same route.
