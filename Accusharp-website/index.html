Quick answers

Knife sharpening FAQs

How much does mobile knife sharpening cost?
Home service is $80 flat for 1 to 8 knives, $10 per knife for 9 to 19 knives, and $8 per knife for 20 or more. Every knife type is the same price. See the full pricing page for travel zones and add-ons.
What areas do you serve?
We cover Los Angeles County, Orange County, Ventura County, and parts of the Inland Empire. Most of LA County and northern Orange County have no travel fee; outer areas carry a $15 to $50 travel charge that we confirm before scheduling.
Do I have to drop off or ship my knives?
No. We drive to your home or restaurant and sharpen everything on-site. Most home appointments take about 30 minutes.
What can you sharpen?
Kitchen and chef knives, paring knives, cleavers, serrated knives, Japanese single-bevel knives, pocket and hunting knives, and scissors and shears.
Do you sharpen for restaurants and commercial kitchens?
Yes. Restaurants, butcher shops, caterers, and food trucks can set up weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly on-site visits at volume pricing. Call (310) 420-1147 for a quote.
How do I book, and when do I pay?
Book with the online form, or call or text (310) 420-1147. We confirm within 24 hours. Payment is due after the work is done: cash, card, Venmo, or Zelle.
Is the work guaranteed?
Yes. If a knife is not razor sharp we re-sharpen it free, and the same applies if an edge fails under normal use within 30 days.
